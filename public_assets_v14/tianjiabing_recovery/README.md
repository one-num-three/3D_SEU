# Tianjiabing roof/type recovery — bounded candidate

Six new meshes across two named OSM components, **not six buildings**. Replace exactly `SEU_B_OSMWAY_300010601.001`; keep the outline and all nine existing south `SEU_B_OSMWAY_300010619__0001`–`__0009` wall/window objects unchanged. Append the six exact names in data/INTEGRATION.json. This is a coordinate-preserving independent patch, not the complete campus.

The north lost legacy wing was much too deep (20.8085 m versus actual mapped 12.5209 m); it is not pasted or squeezed. Its new core follows the accepted OSM polygon, retaining the previous 12 m estimated wall height. School 2021 photos support narrow straight bar + open southern U + blue-grey pitched roof types. Four visible window-row types are represented, **not a claim of four total surveyed floors**. Columns, window dimensions, colours and roof slopes are estimated. North roof ridge14.27 m and southern ridge26.70 m are estimates. South old wall top23.55 m and old six-row windows remain unchanged and still need photo-based height/floor verification. No inferred corridors, doors, vehicles or courtyard fill were added. Therefore this candidate is not a fully photo-reconstructed Tianjiabing complex.

Run `python scripts/prepare.py` with numpy/shapely, then `blender -b -P scripts/build.py`; exact generated vertices/faces/materials also live in data/geometry_database.json.gz. Blender metric local coordinates are EPSG32650 minus origin E671709.6990725585,N3529586.400278158; GLB preserves metres with standard Y-up conversion. Full new projections are supplied for vegetation integration checks. Whole-campus tree clearance remains an integrator check.
